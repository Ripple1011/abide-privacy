Delete Your Account — abide (in Christ)

Last updated: 24 July 2026

This page explains how to delete your abide (in Christ) account and the data associated with it. You can delete your account directly in the app, or request deletion by email.

Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open the abide app and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the menu (⋮) in the top-right corner, then tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are permanently removed.

Delete by email

If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion by emailing us from the email address linked to your account, with the subject “Delete my account”:

Email: churchservicesconnect@gmail.com

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account credentials (sign-in).
  • Your profile details (email, display name, city, church denomination/tradition, language preference).
  • Your saved content (videos, verses, devotions, songs, favourite churches).
  • The prayer requests you posted to the community Prayer Wall.

Deleted data is removed from our active systems immediately. Some data may remain in encrypted backups for a limited period before being automatically overwritten.

For more detail on how we handle your information, see our Privacy Policy.
