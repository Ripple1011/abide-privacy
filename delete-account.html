Delete your account & data

App: abide (in Christ) · Developer: Ripple Macloen

You can permanently delete your abide account and all associated data at any time. There are two ways:

Option 1 — Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open the abide app and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are permanently removed.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you can’t access the app, email us and we will delete your account and data:

Email: rmacloen@gmail.com
Please send from the email address linked to your abide account, with the subject “Delete my account”.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

  • Your account (email/sign-in) and profile (name, city, church denomination, language).
  • Your saved items (saved videos, verses, devotions, favourites).
  • Your prayer requests posted to the Prayer Wall.

Data retention

Deleted data is removed from our active systems immediately. Residual copies may remain in encrypted backups for a limited period before being automatically overwritten. We do not retain your personal data after deletion except where required by law.

See our full Privacy Policy for more details.
